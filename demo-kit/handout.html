Problem & Solution

Konkan's GI-tagged Alphonso mango loses 15–30% of yield annually to disease. Identification is late or wrong; expert advice takes a week and costs money; existing apps are English-first.

  • 📸 Photo → diagnosis — leaf/flower/fruit; disease + severity + confidence
  • 🎙️ Talk, don't type — symptoms by voice (Marathi/Hindi/English)
  • 🔊 Hear the cure — treatment read aloud in Marathi: medicine, exact dosage, spray schedule
  • 🌿 Chemical + organic options, DBSKKV-aligned dosages
  • 📤 Share to WhatsApp — forward the treatment plan to an FPO group or expert
  • ⏰ Rescan reminder — AI suggests the follow-up date; one tap saves it to the phone calendar
  • 📴 Offline safety — last diagnosis cached on-device, clearly flagged when offline
  • 🔐 Private by design — RLS: a farmer sees only their own scans

Honest engineering

Why does it take 5–15 seconds?

That's the real cost of careful vision analysis on the free tier. The scan screen shows rotating status text and a live timer — the wait never looks frozen. We'd rather tell farmers the truth than fake speed.

What if the AI is wrong?

An unclear or non-mango photo gets a low-confidence "send a closer photo" reply — never a confident wrong answer. Every field is validated server-side (severity/stage whitelists, confidence clamping).

What if the network dies?

The last successful diagnosis is cached on the phone; the result screen reopens it with a clear "offline" banner (tested under real network failure). History is never empty: new accounts get 3 clearly-marked sample scans.

Why only Alphonso mango?

Depth beats breadth: one crop, one region, one language — verified with published evidence, unlike 400-crop generic apps. The same engine takes verified "crop packs" to grapes or pomegranate next.

Technology & cost

Stack: React Native (Expo SDK 54, expo-router) · TypeScript · Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Storage + Edge Functions, RLS) · Google Gemini vision + speech (free tier) · expo-speech (Marathi TTS) · WhatsApp share via native share sheet

  • The AI key never ships in the app — all calls go through a server-side edge function; 3-model × 2-key automatic fallback chain + a live /health probe powers the pre-demo status page.
  • Cost: Gemini free tier + Supabase free tier + Expo free = a fraction of a paisa per diagnosis; always free for farmers.
  • Roadmap: median latency < 3s (image downscale + streaming); fully offline-first queue; Konkan outbreak map from anonymized scans; e-NAM mandi prices.

Disclaimer: AI output is advisory guidance, not a prescription. Always follow pesticide labels and confirm with local agriculture officers.
