The Problem

  • Konkan's GI-tagged Alphonso mango loses 15–30% of yield every year to disease — powdery mildew, mango hopper, anthracnose, fruit fly.
  • Identification is late or wrong; expert advice takes a week or more and costs money.
  • Existing apps are English-first — language and literacy are the biggest barriers.

The Solution — one crop, one language, one farmer

📸Photo → diagnosis — leaf / flower / fruit photo; disease + severity + confidence
🎙️Talk, don't type — symptoms by voice (Marathi / Hindi / English)
🔊Hear the cure — the treatment is read aloud in Marathi: medicine, exact dosage, spray schedule
🌿Both paths — chemical AND organic options, DBSKKV-recommended dosages
📴Offline safety — the last diagnosis is saved on the phone; it survives dead networks
📤Share to WhatsApp — forward the full treatment plan to an FPO group or expert
🔐Private by design — row-level security: a farmer sees only their own scans
